Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Mutual Consent Divorce take to complete in Family Court?

A mutual consent divorce (Sec 13B HMA) generally takes around 6 months between the 1st Motion and 2nd Motion, though courts may waive the 6-month period under specific circumstances.

How is monthly maintenance calculated in family disputes? (भरण-पोषण कैसे तय होता है?)

Under Section 125 CrPC / BNSS, courts evaluate the income of both spouses, standard of living during marriage, financial liabilities, and reasonable needs of the dependent spouse and minor children.

What factors determine child custody in a divorce case?

Child custody is decided based on the overall welfare and best interests of the child, considering the child’s age, educational background, emotional bonding, and living environment offered by each parent.

Where are matrimonial and divorce petitions filed in Roorkee?

Matrimonial suits, maintenance petitions, and divorce applications arising in Roorkee are filed before the Family Court having jurisdiction over Roorkee and Haridwar district.

What is Stridhan and how can a woman claim it back?

Stridhan includes all gifts, jewelry, cash, and items presented to a woman before, during, or after marriage. It is her exclusive property, and a petition under Section 406 IPC / DV Act can be filed for its recovery.
