Consumer Rights

Consumer Protection Rights: Filing Complaints for Defective Goods & Service Deficiency

✍️ Advocate RK & Associates📅 2026-08-05⏱️ 4 min read

Who is a Consumer?

Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, a consumer is any person who buys goods or avails services for consideration for personal use.

Common Grounds for Consumer Complaints

- Defective Goods: Manufacturing defects, adulterated items, or non-conformance to warranties. - Deficiency of Service: Negligence or substandard service by banks, insurance companies, builders, airlines, or hospitals. - Unfair Trade Practices: Charging above MRP, misleading advertisements, or refusal to issue tax invoices.

Procedure for Instituting a Consumer Complaint

1. Formal Legal Notice: Issue a 15-day statutory notice demanding replacement, refund, or rectification. 2. District Consumer Commission (DCDRC): File a complaint before the District Commission for claims up to ₹50 Lakhs. 3. Documentation: Attach purchase invoices, warranty cards, correspondence, and technical reports. 4. Reliefs Claimable: Replacement of product, full monetary refund, and compensation for mental agony and litigation costs.

📌 Key Legal Takeaways

  • Consumer complaints must be filed within 2 years from the date the cause of action arose.
  • Compensations can be claimed for mental agony, harassment, and monetary losses.
  • Purchase receipts and written correspondence serve as primary documentary evidence.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can consumer complaints be filed online in India?

Yes, consumers can file complaints electronically through the official e-Daakhil portal (edaakhil.nic.in).
