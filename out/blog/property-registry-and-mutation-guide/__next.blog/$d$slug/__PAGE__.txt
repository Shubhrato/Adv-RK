<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">Registration vs Mutation: Understanding the Difference</h2>When purchasing immovable property in India, two distinct legal processes take place:
1. <strong>Property Registration (Sale Deed):</strong> Transfer of title executed between buyer and seller and registered before the Sub-Registrar Office under the Registration Act, 1908.
2. <strong>Mutation of Title (Khatauni / Revenue Records):</strong> Updating official government revenue records by replacing the previous owner's name with the new purchaser's name.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><hr style="border:0; border-top:1px solid var(--border-color); margin:2.5rem 0;" /><p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">Pre-Purchase Real Estate Due Diligence Checklist</h2>
#<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">1. 30-Year Title Search Verification</h2>Verify the historical chain of title deeds for at least the past 30 years to confirm that the vendor possesses absolute, unencumbered ownership rights.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p>#<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">2. Encumbrance Certificate (EC)</h2>Obtain an Encumbrance Certificate from the Sub-Registrar Office to ensure the property is free from pending bank mortgages, litigation, or monetary liabilities.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p>#<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">3. Revenue Record Verification (Khatauni & Bhu-Naksha)</h2>Cross-examine the Khatauni extract, Khasra numbers, land area, and zoning categories (agricultural vs non-agricultural) with revenue department records.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p>#<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">4. Verification of Pending Family & Court Disputes</h2>Ensure that the land is not subject to family partition suits, ceiling laws, or court injunction orders.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><hr style="border:0; border-top:1px solid var(--border-color); margin:2.5rem 0;" /><p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">The Land Mutation (Dakhl-Kharij) Procedure</h2>After executing the sale deed, file an application for mutation before the Tehsildar Court.
