Revenue Record Verification (Khatauni & Bhu-Naksha)</h2>Cross-examine the Khatauni extract, Khasra numbers, land area, and zoning categories (agricultural vs non-agricultural) with revenue department records.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p>#<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">4.
