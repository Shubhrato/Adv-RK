Mutual Consent Divorce (Section 13B HMA)</h2>When both spouses mutually agree to dissolve the marriage amicably:
- <strong>Pre-requisites:</strong> Spouses must have lived separately for a minimum period of 1 year.
- <strong>Procedure:</strong> Joint petition filed for 1st Motion, followed by 2nd Motion.
- <strong>Cooling-Off Period:</strong> Statutory 6-month period between motions, subject to judicial waiver under specific conditions.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p>#<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">2. Contested Divorce</h2>Filed unilaterally by one spouse based on statutory grounds under Section 13(1) HMA:
- Physical or Mental Cruelty
- Desertion (continuous separation for 2+ years)
- Adultery or Unsoundness of Mind<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><hr style="border:0; border-top:1px solid var(--border-color); margin:2.5rem 0;" /><p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">Maintenance & Financial Rights (Sec 125 CrPC / BNSS)</h2>Indian matrimonial law safeguards financial maintenance for spouses and dependent children:
- <strong>Interim Maintenance:</strong> Monthly maintenance awarded during the pendency of court proceedings.
- <strong>Permanent Alimony:</strong> Lump-sum or recurring monthly alimony awarded upon final decree.
- <strong>Stridhan Protection:</strong> All personal gifts, jewelry, and assets gifted to the wife belong exclusively to her and can be legally reclaimed.
