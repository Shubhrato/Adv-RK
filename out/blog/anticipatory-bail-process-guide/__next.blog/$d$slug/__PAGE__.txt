<h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">What is Anticipatory Bail?</h2>Anticipatory bail is a pre-arrest legal remedy granted under <strong>Section 438 CrPC / Section 482 BNSS</strong>. When an individual apprehends arrest on accusations of having committed a non-bailable offence, they may apply to the court for a direction that, in the event of such arrest, they shall be released on bail.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><hr style="border:0; border-top:1px solid var(--border-color); margin:2.5rem 0;" /><p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">Categories of Bail in Criminal Procedure</h2>
1. <strong>Regular Bail (Sec 437/439):</strong> Applied for when an accused person is already under police or judicial custody.
2. <strong>Anticipatory Bail (Sec 438):</strong> Applied for prior to arrest upon reasonable apprehension of prosecution.
3. <strong>Interim Bail:</strong> Temporary bail granted pending the final disposal of a regular or anticipatory bail application.<p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><hr style="border:0; border-top:1px solid var(--border-color); margin:2.5rem 0;" /><p style="margin-bottom:1.5rem; color:var(--text-muted);"></p><h2 style="font-size:1.6rem; color:var(--primary-color); margin-top:2rem; margin-bottom:1rem; font-family:var(--font-heading);">Procedure for Applying for Anticipatory Bail</h2>- <strong>Jurisdiction:</strong> Filed before the Sessions Court or the High Court.
- <strong>Key Grounds:</strong> Absence of criminal antecedents, false implication, cooperation with police investigation, and assurance not to tamper with evidence.
- <strong>Mandatory Conditions:</strong> Courts usually impose conditions prohibiting international travel, requiring attendance at the police station when summoned, and forbidding contact with prosecution witnesses.
